    Claude API key

    Why a key: Anthropic doesn't offer a "sign in with Claude" for apps like this one, so Maxie uses an API key from your own account. Get one at console.anthropic.com/settings/keys (you add a payment method under Billing).

    What it costs: Haiku 5.5 is $0.10 in and $0.50 out per million tokens, Sonnet 5.5 is $2 and $10, Opus 5.5 is $4 and $20, and a web search is $0.01 (Anthropic pricing, October 2026). Most commands never reach Claude, and the cached prompt keeps the rest cheap. My estimate for everyday use is $5 to $10 a month (README, Costs). The cap below stops it at a number you pick.

    Without a key: rule commands still work (open an app or link, music, volume, timers, maths, dates, the time, remember this), and so do Memory, Activity and Settings.

    Google

    Gmail, Calendar and Drive, through Google's own sign-in. You can connect more than one account. Google doesn't let an open source app ship a shared client secret, so you make your own OAuth client once. It's free and takes about five minutes.

    1. Open Google Cloud and create a project (any name, for example "Maxie").
    2. In APIs and Services, Library, enable the Gmail API, the Google Calendar API and the Google Drive API.
    3. Open Google Auth Platform, choose External, fill in an app name and your email. Under Audience, add your own Google account as a test user, or publish the app so the sign-in doesn't expire after 7 days. Google will warn that the app isn't verified; that's expected for an app only you use.
    4. Under Clients, create a client of type Desktop app. Desktop clients accept the sign-in coming back to this computer (http://127.0.0.1:8780/oauth/google/callback) without any extra setup.
    5. Download its JSON and paste it below, or paste the client ID and secret.

    Accounts

    "main" is your own mail, Calendar and Drive: Maxie reads, searches and drafts, and sends only after your OK. "job" is a read-only inbox the morning brief scans for interviews and recruiters. Any other label is another read-only inbox. Scopes are the smallest that do the job: Gmail read and compose, Calendar events, Drive read-only.

          A small local classifier that sits between the regex rules and Claude Haiku. It's free and private, but it's a 421M-parameter model you run yourself with make laya (README, "Local router"). Leave the URL empty to skip it.

          Job search apps

          jobhunt and resumegen are two companion apps I wrote for my own job search. Maxie can read new jobs and queue tailored resumes through their APIs. If you don't run them, leave this empty.

          Backups

          Nightly encrypted backups of your memory and settings (age, with a passphrase). Keep the passphrase somewhere safe: without it a backup can't be opened.
